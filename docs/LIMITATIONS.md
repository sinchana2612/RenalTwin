# Limitations
- No open, verified, linked real longitudinal CKD cohort was identified in the blueprint.
- The model is trained only on synthetic simulator data; metrics are not clinical evidence.
- Sensor observations are synthetic and are not used by the primary model.
- UCI CKD is cross-sectional and lacks sex; it cannot support the longitudinal target.
- MIMIC-IV demo is a small ICU dataset, not a general CKD cohort.
- No external validation, prospective validation, or clinical-use authorization.
